## About Living Earth

Living Earth shows our planet in motion, with satellite clouds refreshed every hour, sunrise sweeping across the globe and city lights coming on as darkness falls.

It doubles as a world clock and weather app, with the time and weather in each saved city, plus hourly and 10-day forecasts. Maps show temperature, humidity and wind, and the app follows hurricanes and typhoons around the world, with a notification when one forms or changes strength.

Living Earth also tracks solar and lunar eclipses and works as a bedside clock with a Wake Alarm. It's a one-time purchase, with no subscriptions, ads or tracking.

## About the developer

Living Earth is the work of independent developer Moshen Chan of Radiantlabs in San Francisco. A coder by day and an amateur astronomer and astrophotographer by night, Moshen keeps building and refining the app for the love of space and weather.

## What's new in 4.50

Living Earth 4.50 is a free update for existing users, and the first version for iPhone Duo.

- **iPhone Duo.** Unfold it and the Earth spreads across the inner display. Close it to see the globe, clock and weather on the cover screen. Fold it into a tent for a bedside alarm clock. Wake Alarms use Apple's AlarmKit, which lights the inner display with a soft glow when an alarm rings.
- **A more detailed globe.** Zoom in to see sharper clouds, terrain and city lights.

<img src="/press/living-earth-ipad-california.jpg" width="1440" height="1080" loading="lazy" alt="Living Earth on iPad over California at dusk, with clouds off the coast, the Sierra Nevada and the weather in western cities">

- **Borders and place names** appear as you get closer. Tap a city's name to see the weather there.

<img src="/press/living-earth-ipad-mexico.jpg" width="1440" height="1080" loading="lazy" alt="Living Earth on iPad over Mexico, with a hurricane off the Pacific coast, country borders and names, and the weather in Mexican cities">

- **Search** finds any place on Earth and zooms the globe in to show its weather.
- **Expanded daily forecasts.** View hourly conditions for each day in the 10-day forecast, with full charts in Details.
- **Also:** new controls for Search, Layers, Storms and Weather Labels, a Tip Jar, and better support for VoiceOver, Switch Control and larger text. It opens faster, too.
